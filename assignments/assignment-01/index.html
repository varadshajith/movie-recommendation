Reel

Pick your next film by swiping

Reel is a movie and TV show recommender. You swipe through posters and the app learns your taste.

Features

  • Swipe deck of movies and TV shows
  • Personal taste profile for every user
  • Watchlist and "Seen & loved" shelves
  • Trailer and cast in the detail view

How to use it

  1. Create an account and log in.
  2. Pick a few genres you like.
  3. Choose 3–5 titles you have already loved.
  4. Start swiping:
    • Right = Like
    • Left = Nope
    • Up = Meh (comes back after 30 days)
    • Down or tap = More info
